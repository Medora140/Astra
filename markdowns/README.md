Sample defence/technology PDFs for **Challenge 01: AI-Powered Defence
Document Intelligence System**.

|---|---|---|---|
| `sample-documents/unmanned-aerial-vehicle-overview.pdf` | Unmanned aerial vehicle | 50 | Wikipedia |

Machine-readable metadata: `sample-metadata.json`.
Suggested questions your system should answer: `example-questions.md`.

## Why these documents

- Real text PDFs (not scanned images) so extraction works out of the box.
- Different lengths (12 → 50 pages) so you test pagination and long-input
  behaviour.
- Defence/technology topics matching the challenge example (unmanned
  aerial systems).

## Notes

- Wikipedia is continuously updated; the PDFs are snapshots retrieved on
  the date in `sample-metadata.json`.
- You may use your own documents instead — that is allowed and sometimes
  better. The starter set exists so everyone *can* start with zero setup.
